# Surface brief: index.html (the whole single-page site)

Scope: the one public page at https://aaryanp35.vercel.app. Visitor mode: Persuade (a recruiter decides whether to keep reading, then reaches for the resume or email).

Audience and job: technical recruiters and hiring managers screening for 2027 APM, data science, and AI engineering roles, often on a phone, deciding in seconds. Action: download the resume, send an email, open a project. Proof: real roles with dates, four headline projects clearly explained in broad terms, public repos and a live app, athletics as character. Constraints: no specific metrics, dollar figures, or data results on the page (they live in the resume); not fintech-flavored; confidential work at resume level only; no phone number on the page (the owner keeps it in the PDF); works without JavaScript; respects reduced motion; 360px wide holds.

Chosen direction and memorable moment: the page is a drive south on Interstate 280 from San Francisco to Menlo Park, built from the Caltrans sign system. The memorable moment is the name on an overhead guide sign with Karl the Fog over the city, then golden hills, Crystal Springs, and the Stanford dish sliding past as the mile counter climbs to the Sand Hill Road exit.

History: an earlier round rolled and locked a topographic quadrangle sheet (seed below). Six renditions were built, including three topo variants, a Mt Tamalpais climb, and this drive. A recruiter review scored the drive highest (80.9 of 100) and the owner chose it as the shipping base, with grafts from the other renditions.

Unresolved: Proxii repo rename; whether an Athenaeum public repo gets linked later.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the drive home, read like the road. It owns the idea that a career can be signed the way a highway is signed, plainly and in order, and refuses the category default of a centered hero with metric chips, pill buttons, and a grid of identical project cards.

OWN-WORLD: Caltrans and MUTCD sign system as the committed material. Guide-sign green panels with white legend type, a white border inset, and exit tabs for roles; brown recreation and cultural panels for points of interest and the vista-point photo; blue service panels for rest-area content (athletics, education, skills); one advisory white plate for Karl the Fog. Sign type is Overpass (the Highway Gothic descendant); body is Source Serif 4; miles and dates are a measurement mono. Ground is daylight sky over layered SVG Peninsula hills, golden grass over oak-dark folds, the Crystal Springs reservoir in blue, fog white over the city. Sign yellow is the single action color, used only for Resume and Email. A truss gantry carries the hero sign. Four type sizes.

STORY: The visitor merges at mile 0, reads in three seconds that Aaryan is an Associate PM at DeUNA and builds AI systems for defense wargaming at Strand Analytica, sees his face at the vista point, and sees the four headline points of interest ahead. Driving south, they pass the selected work first (DeUNA, Athenaeum, Strand Analytica, Proxii, then the rest), then the experience exits in order, then rest-area panels on payments and defense, rowing, education and skills, and exit at Sand Hill Road where resume and email are the final sign. They leave able to name his role, two projects, and one thing about him.
